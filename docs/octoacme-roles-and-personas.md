# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation throughout the project lifecycle. They collaborate with developers and product managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria alignment
- Plan and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Triage and prioritize defects
- Manage test automation and CI/CD quality gates
- Report on quality metrics and risk assessments
- Conduct user acceptance testing (UAT) coordination

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features with confidence
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Enable fast, reliable deployments

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Quality status in weekly syncs
- Test plans and defect reports
- Release readiness assessments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, code review for testability, and defect triage
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and define quality expectations
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and flag release readiness risks

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design guidance, and mentorship. They balance feature velocity with technical excellence, manage technical debt, and ensure system scalability and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Mentor junior developers and facilitate knowledge sharing
- Identify and prioritize technical debt
- Ensure code quality, performance, and security standards
- Assess technical feasibility and propose solutions
- Guide integration and dependency management

### Goals
- Maintain system reliability and scalability
- Build a strong, learning-oriented engineering culture
- Balance feature delivery with long-term system health

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Technical risk identification in planning and syncs
- Retrospectives focused on process and capability improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide design guidance, conduct code reviews, mentor on technical excellence
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks, dependencies, and effort estimates

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and foster a culture of continuous improvement. They coach teams on agile practices and help optimize delivery workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and escalate impediments
- Coach team on agile practices and self-organization
- Track velocity and metrics to identify process improvements
- Support retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Advocate for psychological safety and team well-being

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, high-velocity delivery
- Foster a culture of learning and continuous improvement
- Reduce process friction and rework

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- One-on-ones with team members for feedback and coaching
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Remove blockers, facilitate collaboration, coach on self-organization
- **With Product Managers**: Support backlog refinement and sprint planning collaboration
- **With Project Managers**: Complement project coordination with agile ceremony facilitation and team health focus

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and resource allocation. They represent customer needs, business priorities, and organizational constraints to the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve budget, timeline, and scope trade-offs
- Provide executive air cover and remove organizational blockers
- Communicate project status to leadership and key stakeholders
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Advocate for the project and secure resources

### Goals
- Ensure project delivery aligns with business strategy
- Reduce rework and scope creep through clear prioritization
- Maximize business value and stakeholder satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Monthly/quarterly stakeholder briefings
- Milestone reviews and decision gates
- Escalation for resource conflicts or strategic alignment issues
- Release announcements and business impact communication

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and success metrics
- **With Project Managers**: Approve scope, budget, and timeline decisions; provide escalation support
- **With Developers**: Provide business context and visibility on impact
